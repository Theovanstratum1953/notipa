Install the app

Installing Notipa puts it on your home screen or app list like any other app — full screen, no browser address bar, and it opens straight to your dashboard.

Your browser already has Notipa installed, or doesn't support one-click install here — look for an "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" option in your browser's menu instead.

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later and only work once Notipa is installed this way — opening it from a home-screen icon, not just a browser tab.
